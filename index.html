Privacy Policy for ShonaApp: Dambamazwi

Last Updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to ShonaApp: Dambamazwi! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application ("App"). By using ShonaApp: Dambamazwi, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve your gaming experience:

  • Account & Profile Data: A locally stored player name and chosen avatar/totem. We do not require email or social media login to play.
  • Gameplay Data: Your scores, words found, longest words, achievement progress, daily challenge participation, and streak counts. This is stored locally on your device and synced to our secure servers to enable multiplayer and leaderboard features.
  • Device Information: Anonymous device identifiers, operating system version, and app crash logs (via Firebase Crashlytics) to ensure app stability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App's features (e.g., Daily Challenges, Royal Multiplayer Mode, Leaderboards).
  • To track your progress, streaks, and in-game currency (Diamonds).
  • To diagnose technical issues and prevent crashes.
  • To serve rewarded video ads (if applicable), which are optional and used to grant in-game rewards.

3. Third-Party Services

ShonaApp: Dambamazwi uses trusted third-party services to operate. These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for real-time database syncing (leaderboards, multiplayer lobbies), analytics, and crash reporting. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Ad Networks (e.g., Google AdMob): Used only if you choose to watch rewarded video ads for in-game currency. Ad networks may collect anonymous device IDs to serve relevant ads. Google Privacy Policy

4. Data Security

We take your privacy seriously. All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS). We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Children's Privacy

ShonaApp: Dambamazwi is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected such data, we will take steps to delete it.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request the deletion of your data. Since most gameplay data is tied to an anonymous Firebase ID, you can delete your local app data or uninstall the app. For server-side data deletion requests, please contact us at the email below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
